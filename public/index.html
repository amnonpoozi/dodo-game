Quick rules
  • On your turn: raise the bid, call Dodo (the bid is a lie), or call Believe (the bid is exactly right).
  • A bid is a quantity and a face, e.g. Three 4s = “at least three dice count as 4” across all players.
  • 1s are wildcards in normal rounds — unless the bid itself is on 1s.
  • Switching to a bid on 1s: quantity must be > half the previous quantity (floor(prev/2)+1). Switching back from 1s: double the quantity.
  • Check: only with exactly 5 dice forming Five of a Kind, All Different, or Full House. It skips your turn; the bid stays.
  • Blind Round: when a player drops to exactly 1 die. Nobody sees any dice; the lone die sets a secret target; bids are quantity only; 1s are not wild.
